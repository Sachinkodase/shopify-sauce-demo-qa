## 7. Risks and Limitations

- Testing was performed on the publicly available Shopify Sauce Demo website.
- Website functionality or product data may change without prior notice.
- Third-party payment processing and backend services were outside the scope of testing.
- Testing was limited to manual functional testing and did not include performance, security, or automated testing.

- ---

## 8. Overall Assessment

The Shopify Sauce Demo application performed well during functional testing. All planned test cases were executed, and the majority passed successfully. One medium-severity defect was identified in the Cart module and documented separately. No critical or high-severity issues were found that would prevent users from completing the primary application workflows.

---

## 9. Recommendation

Based on the completed functional testing activities, the Shopify Sauce Demo application is considered functionally stable for demonstration purposes. The identified medium-severity defect should be addressed in a future update; however, it does not significantly impact the core user experience. No critical issues were identified that would prevent normal application usage.
